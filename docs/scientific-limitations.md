# Scientific limitations

Read this before interpreting anything PocketFly shows you.

## The stack PocketFly actually runs

```
real connectome STRUCTURE
+ simplified LIF dynamics (computational parameters)
+ artificial sensory encoding (one synthetic "photoreceptor" per side)
+ artificial action decoding (hand-built difference/threshold decoders)
+ artificial reward bookkeeping
```

Each layer is a modeling choice made for interactivity, not for biological
fidelity.

## What follows from that

1. **Connectome wiring is not a mind.** A wiring diagram plus generic
   dynamics does not reproduce the computations the real circuit performs.
   The same structure with different parameters, neurotransmitter classes,
   or time constants can behave entirely differently.

2. **Behavior in games is not fly behavior.** When the fly in Light Chase
   turns toward the light, that is the difference decoder acting on
   asymmetrical structure-driven activity. It demonstrates that the loop
   works; it does not demonstrate that real Drosophila chase lights this
   way.

3. **The sample brain is not the fly CNS.** The bundled 1,024-neuron brain
   is synthetic, generated by `data/tools/generate_sample.py` with an
   ipsilateral-dominant wiring bias *we chose*. Any "left/right bias" it
   shows is partly designed in, not discovered.

4. **Sensory encoding is not vision.** Two scalar channels stand in for a
   compound eye with thousands of ommatidia, motion detection, and color.
   The channel contract exists so real encoders can be added; nothing in
   v0.1 should be read as visual processing.

5. **The dynamics are not electrophysiology.** One step is not one
   millisecond; thresholds and decays are not measured parameters;
   inhibition is absent; no neuron model includes ion channels,
   adaptation, or neuromodulation.

6. **Ablation results are suggestions, not findings.** Group-level, on a
   simplified model, with artificial I/O — useful for building intuition
   about the graph, not evidence about the animal.

7. **Experiments measure the model, not the animal.** The stimulus
   protocols produce data about *this software's behavior under these
   parameters*. They are teaching and exploration tools.

## What PocketFly IS good for

- Feeling the scale and structure of a connectome-driven system on a device
  you carry.
- Building intuition for propagation: pause, step, ablate, watch.
- A real, testable closed loop for experimenting with encoders, decoders,
  rules, and environments — all swappable, all inspectable.
- A scaffold that can accept real datasets (converted `.pflybrain`) and
  better dynamics without redesign.

## Claim discipline

The README, UI, and this document deliberately avoid saying that a fly's
brain "runs" on a phone. What runs is a **computational model built on
connectome structure**. Where the UI shows neural activity, it is the
activity of this model, labeled *Simplified neural dynamics*.
